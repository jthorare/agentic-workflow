**canvas-40**: The Fit floor is a font size: Fit stops where card text reaches 12 px (12/14 = 86% at 100% text, 12/18.2 = 66% at 130%), so it stops at the floor (86%) and the canvas scrolls; the scrollbar and the minimap box show where you are. The map shows only when the graph does not fit (it replaces "a minimap from 12 steps up"); while it shows it is an overlay, and the scroll extent has bottom padding of map height + 16 (bottom only) so any card or edge can scroll clear of it. This shot is at the end of the scroll, where the map sits over the padding. Wrap is computed at the floor zoom and at canvas width / Fit zoom, so Fit never scrolls sideways (vertical bar only, hScroll=0). The zoom control is a 56 px band under the canvas on every frame; the canvas clip above it is 728 px at 1440x900. Measure: 22 of 40 cards fully in view; cut: none.
