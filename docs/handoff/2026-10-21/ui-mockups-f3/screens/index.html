Sindri: workflow redesign prototype

A clickable model of the new Sindri screens, filled with real Sindri data and written in plain words. The look copies SuperPlane for the workflow canvas, Linear for dense lists and HumanLayer for approvals. Nothing here is saved: every button only shows what would happen.

Guided tour

Twelve stops, about five minutes. Each stop has a Next button at the bottom of the screen.

  1. 1BoardSee every run in its stage. Waiting runs say so on the card, and the filter above counts them.workflows.html
  2. 2ListThe same runs as dense rows, grouped by workflow, in Linear's style.workflows.html
  3. 3Open a runThe run as a canvas of stages. The side panel explains the selected step and why it matters.run-canvas.html
  4. 4A failed runWhere it stopped, what the reviewer found, and what nothing reached.run-canvas.html
  5. 5Didn't work out, and CancelledEnded without a result is neutral, not red. The Dark mode toggle run shows Cancelled.run-canvas.html
  6. 6Needs you: approve the scopeThe whole request in one place, then approve, send back or drop.needs-you.html
  7. 7Send back with a reasonSending back needs a reason. Ctrl+Enter sends it.needs-you.html
  8. 8Take over and hand backTake over pauses the clock. Hand back resumes it.needs-you.html
  9. 9Context mapWhat Sindri knows around this item, and why each part is in the map.context.html
  10. 10File mapThe same context as files and folders.context.html
  11. 11Setup: the two approvalsApprove your settings, then hold to approve all new workflows.setup.html
  12. 12Questions for the ownerEvery open decision, with the recommended answer and the app it copies.questions.html

Annotations (top right, on every screen) outlines what needs new data from Sindri. Turn it on to see which parts of a screen the API does not serve yet.
