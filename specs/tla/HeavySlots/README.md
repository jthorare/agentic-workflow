# HeavySlots

- **Models:** the box-wide heavy-job slots: `<locks>/heavy-job.lock` (slot 1) and `heavy-job.lock.k` (slot k), mkdir mutexes shared by three takers.
  - The `heavy` wrapper, `scripts/heavy.sh`. `acquire box-wide` reads the cap file and the meta reserve on every pass (`config/lib/heavy-config.sh`, #2061). It does mkdir, then the owner write. To reclaim a slot it moves `owner` aside to `owner.reclaim.<its pid>`, checks it is the dead line it read, then removes it and the folder (the same rename-aside as locks.sh; before #2058 it unlinked and rmdired with no mutex).
  - `config/lib/locks.sh` `acquire_any_lock`: on the heavy-job lock only, reads the cap file (it wins over the slot count it was given) and the reserve on every pass through the same `heavy-config.sh`, and a non-meta taker skips the reserved slots (#2061); every other lock counts the slots it is given. Its record is `<dir>/pid`. To reclaim, it moves the record aside first (`_lock_reclaim_dead`).
  - `DirectoryHeavySlots.cs`: reads the cap and the reserve on every pass (`HeavyLockSettings.CurrentSlots` / `CurrentReserve`, over `HeavyCap`); a Sindri job is never meta, so it skips the reserved slots (#2061). Its record is `<dir>/owner`, and it reclaims by renaming the record aside (`Claim`, to `owner.reclaim.<its pid>.<random>`) and then a non-recursive delete. `HeavySlotLease` leaves alone a slot whose owner names another process.

  All three free a slot left holding only a moved-aside record (`*.reclaim.<pid>…`) whose mover is dead: a reclaimer SIGKILLed between its move and its delete (#2065).

  Every taker works to the cap and the reserve as the files read now; the model lets the read and the mkdir after it be one step (and the retry mkdir after a reclaim re-checks the cap), where the code reads once per pass and then tries every slot, a reclaim included: a window of one pass, milliseconds. Each syscall is one step; the move aside and the re-read are two. Simpler than the code: a release checks the job (`who`), where Sindri's `IsOurs` / `HeavySlotLease` compare only the pid, which every job in one serve shares (matters with 2+ Sindri jobs). A process can be SIGKILLed at any step: its folder and record stay, and its pid is never reused. Only SIGKILL is modelled: a reclaimer whose read or delete fails in process (Sindri's `Claim` puts the record back), a reused pid, and the 6 h bound every taker puts on a leftover whose reclaimer looks alive are covered by tests, not TLC. Every taker reads both `pid` and `owner`, and frees a folder with no record once its mkdir-er is dead (the code waits a minute by mtime; the model has an `Age` step that makes a made-or-emptied folder old, and assumes a live process finishes its rmdir within that minute). Not modelled: the per-session `heavy-session/<pid>.lock` pool, the memory guard (it only skips slots), timeouts, the 6 h held-too-long grace and the TS `holder.json`.
- **Checks:** `OneHolderPerSlot` (no two live processes hold one slot folder) and `SomeoneRuns` (the box never locks up among the live jobs). `TakeWithinCap` (nobody takes a slot above the cap file as it reads at that moment) and `ReserveHonored` (a non-meta job never takes a slot meta reserved) are in `HeavySlots.cfg` too, but there the cap never changes and no job is meta; they bite in `StaleCap.cfg` (the cap is lowered from 2 to 1 while jobs wait: no taker takes slot 2 afterwards) and `IgnoredReserve.cfg` (slot 1 reserved: no non-meta wrapper, locks.sh or Sindri job takes it). Before #2061 both failed and sat in `gaps/` as witnesses. `LeftoverCleared` (a record a dead reclaimer moved aside is always removed again) is in `LeftoverReclaim.cfg`, and `OrphanReclaimed` (a slot whose holder died is freed, whichever taker wrote its record, or none) in `DeadHolderKept.cfg`. `NoStarvation` (every waiter gets a slot) holds in `FullReserve.cfg`: a lone lane job against a reserve file as big as the slot count still gets the slot, because every reader caps the reserve at the slot count minus one (#2060; before, it sat in `gaps/`). It fails as a witness on `gaps/PollStarvation.cfg`.
- **Bounds:** `HeavySlots.cfg`: one wrapper job, one locks.sh holder, one Sindri job, 2 slots, every cap 2, no reserve, no SIGKILL, weak fairness; about 2k states. `HeavySlotsReclaim.cfg`: two locks.sh holders and one Sindri job on 1 slot, 1 SIGKILL anywhere. Their rename-aside reclaims never let two processes hold one slot (about 1k states). `WrapperReclaimRace.cfg` (three wrapper jobs, 1 slot, 1 SIGKILL) and `MixedReclaimRace.cfg` (two wrapper jobs and a locks.sh holder) check the same for the wrapper (#2058). `LeftoverReclaim.cfg`: one of each taker on 1 slot, 2 SIGKILLs (#2065). `DeadHolderKept.cfg`: one wrapper job and one locks.sh holder on 1 slot, 1 SIGKILL; `OrphanReclaimed` holds (#2059). `FullReserve.cfg`: one non-meta wrapper job, 1 slot, reserve file 1, strong fairness (#2060).
