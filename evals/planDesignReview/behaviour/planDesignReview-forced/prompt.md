Plan: 'Reorder my reading list' for a book club tracker. A member's list is a vertical stack of cover cards; to reorder, you drag a card with the mouse or finger and drop it elsewhere. That is the only way to reorder. The list page shows a spinner while loading. Nothing is written about what a brand-new member with zero books sees, what happens when saving the new order fails, or what a screen reader announces. Success: the card settles into place. Copy: the buttons say 'Add book' and 'Remove'.
